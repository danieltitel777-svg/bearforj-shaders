Bearforj particle sprites, generated by tools/bearforj/gen_particle_textures.py.
Procedural. No third-party images, no photographed textures.
atlas.dds is 2048x2048, sixteen 512x512 cells, uncompressed BGRA8 with mipmaps.
Sprite 0 is unused here: SSPP draws a procedural quad when the atlas is missing
or the particle has no sprite id.
Ids: 1 rain, 2 snow, 3 broad leaf, 4 narrow leaf, 5 needle, 6 twig, 7 spark,
8 ember, 9 ash, 10 ice, 11 dust, 12 blood drop, 13 water drop, 14 pollen,
15 butterfly, 16 fly.
