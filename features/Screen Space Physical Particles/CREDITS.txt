Screen-space physical particles are adapted from D7ry's Community Shaders branch
dev/dtry/sspp at commit c9663e0222f97748fd530fef1abd09fd9affd767.
https://github.com/D7ry/skyrim-community-shaders

Bearforj keeps that blood and leaf simulation and adds tree-only leaves, magic
sparks, grass fluff, dandelions, glowing mushroom spores, fireflies, and moths.
New particle art is 36 images packed into three 2048 DXT5 straight-alpha
atlases: leaves_atlas.dds, sparks_atlas.dds, and small_atlas.dds. Those files
are added at integration and are not in this package. They belong in
Data\Shaders\PhysicalParticles\. Cell layout and region tints are in
Data\Shaders\PhysicalParticles\particles.json.
