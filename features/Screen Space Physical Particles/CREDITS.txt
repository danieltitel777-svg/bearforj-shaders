Screen-space physical particles are adapted from D7ry's Community Shaders branch
dev/dtry/sspp at commit c9663e0222f97748fd530fef1abd09fd9affd767.
https://github.com/D7ry/skyrim-community-shaders

Bearforj keeps that blood and leaf simulation and adds tree-only leaves, magic
sparks, grass fluff, dandelions, glowing mushroom spores, fireflies, and moths.
The 36 source images are packed into three 2048 DXT5 straight-alpha atlases:
leaves_atlas.dds, sparks_atlas.dds, and small_atlas.dds, under
Data\Shaders\PhysicalParticles\. Each source PNG is 2048 square. Cells are 512
(leaves and small) or 1024 (sparks), so the packer integer-box downscales them.
Mips are built per cell. Cell layout and region tints are in particles.json.
The generator is tools/bearforj/build_particle_atlases.py.
