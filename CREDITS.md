## Community Shaders

Shader platform, feature buffer, and the features that were already on `bearforj/1-base`.
https://github.com/doodlum/skyrim-community-shaders

## Open Shaders (alandtse)

Character rain drops and streaks (`CharacterRainSpots`), and the wetness ideas this package keeps from that work.
https://github.com/alandtse/open-shaders

## Bottled Shaders

The shader base this branch is integrated onto, including Advanced Skin, Unified Water, Raindrops.png, and the FidelityFX binaries that were already in the base. Those binaries were not replaced.

## Jiaye

Wetness timings used by Advanced Skin: WetFadeTime 10.0, StartSweat 0.75, FullSweat 0.15. Weather transition speed 3.0, slider 0.2–8.0. One shared wetness value per actor, including head and hair.

## D7ry

Screen-space physical particles, physical blood, and the leaf simulation, from `dev/dtry/sspp` at `c9663e0222f97748fd530fef1abd09fd9affd767`.
https://github.com/D7ry/skyrim-community-shaders

## Elemental Contact

Shore, crest, actor, and flow foam, the wet-hair look (`hair_sss.dds`), and the water-wave shore helpers. This package does not include the Elemental Contact menu DLL. Calm open water has no foam. Spray uses `sparks_atlas.dds`.

Windswept files are not part of this package.
